## Binding/path coverage

- **D1:** pervasive across every authenticated route and all scheduled/email/workflow paths (auth, tenant scope, repositories, audit, rate limits, budget authority). Reads and writes are observed by `observeD1`, but observation is not admission.
- **R2:** ticket/article bodies, customer/staff attachments, knowledge files, inbound email attachments, retention cleanup, and outbound-email attachment reads. Tenant adapters provide operation observation; local-beta upload charge is only a beta guard.
- **Durable Objects:** NotificationDO realtime/broadcast/alarm; BudgetCoordinatorDO and BudgetGrantHolderDO for the two API mutation admission path; session budget authority is a current-credential gate for prepared work, not realtime admission.
- **AI:** widget chat, staff knowledge AI suggestion, knowledge embeddings and vectorization workflow. No active #64 AI reservation or model/unit conversion.
- **Vectorize:** knowledge indexing/search/cleanup and widget/staff retrieval; workflow callbacks execute these operations. No admitted dimension envelope.
- **Workflow:** `VECTORIZE_WORKFLOW.create` is invoked by knowledge mutations and executes later in `VectorizeWorkflow.run`; no budget admission at enqueue or step execution.
- **KV:** no `KVNamespace` binding or direct KV operation was found in active application code. Rate limiting is in-memory (`rate-limiter.ts`) or D1 (`SqlRequestLimitRepository`), so do not assign KV dimensions.
- **Email/external:** Resend transport is used by customer auth links and staff ticket replies; automation rules can issue allowlisted HTTPS webhooks; `CloudflareService.getUsageStats` (`GET /api/settings/usage`) calls the Cloudflare GraphQL Analytics API. These external request/provider units and retries are not metered/admitted. No claim is made that returned analytics rows are provider metering for admission.
