# #64 budget coordinator foundation — 11 September 2026

The accepted pure tenant transition model remains at `apps/server/src/budgets/coordinator-state.ts`. It provides JSON-serializable, all-or-nothing generic `ResourceAmounts` grants; holder binding; idempotency-envelope conflicts; separate new-work and recovery pools; revision fencing; expiry/lost-grant uncertainty; terminal-evidence reconciliation; and policy/restriction replacement. Grants end at the earliest selected interval boundary or policy maximum lifetime. `ResourceAmounts` are non-negative safe-integer units. The model retains expired, lost, revoked, uncertain and consumed charges; expiry never returns capacity or a record slot. A measured overrun is retained as actual accounted usage, records a defect and blocks admission rather than being clamped.

This slice adds `BudgetCoordinatorDO` and `owner-aggregate.ts`. A coordinator contains up to 128 server-derived tenant allocations that share one owner allocation set, and retains at most 4,096 grant records in total. Its one Durable Object state is the atomic persistence boundary for tenant and owner reservation/reconciliation transitions: every owner dimension, window and purpose is charged across every participating tenant before a new grant is committed. The coordinator rejects a tenant allocation that does not derive from the configured owner policy or tries to raise its limit, change its allocation/window, or change the recovery split. It retains a global capacity defect if any tenant reports an overrun, so it blocks unsafe admission across that shared owner allocation. Its internal reservation namespaces are server-issued and unique; they are never request fields.

`BudgetAuthorityRepository` now loads deployment-owned policy and tenant-allocation rows from D1. It first checks the supplied `VerifiedTenantScope` against the current `users` tenant, role and session-version row. Its one bounded D1 batch returns at most 129 allocations and revalidates the same deployment authority revision, owner policy revision, coordinator, lease value and allocation count, so it cannot assemble an aggregate from different policies or revisions. The lease is the deployment-owned `authority_max_age_ms`, not a universal 30-second value; it must be a positive safe integer no greater than that owner policy's `maxGrantLifetimeMs`. New grants end at the earlier of policy/window and this configured authority lease.

`BudgetCoordinatorService` is a non-active integration contract only: it requires a future handler to supply a current-authorization gate that rechecks credential expiry/session and capability before budget admission, then derives authority fields and the Durable Object name itself. It has no route or client-selected tenant/policy/address parameter. A D1 authority revision transition preserves every prior charge and marks outstanding grants uncertain before it applies the new active owner/tenant limits; historical allocations remain available only to reconciliation. A D1 revocation freezes admission without returning any accepted charge. A matching policy/restriction revision does not prove freshness: the coordinator rejects new admission at lease expiry until a coherent fresh D1 read renews the unchanged snapshot.

`BudgetGrantHolderDO` provides the durable warm path. It never calls the central coordinator for a warm spend. The current service is intentionally not wired into an application path pending root review of the authorization and envelope contract.
