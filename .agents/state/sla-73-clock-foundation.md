Deliberate pure-module limits: instants and configured exception dates use Gregorian years 0100–9999; schedules use minute precision and same-day windows (overnight work is split by the caller); exceptions are capped at 3,660, daily windows at 48, pause intervals at 4,096, and caller-provided evaluation limits at 3,660 calendar dates / 65,536 intervals. The timezone formatter cache is capped at 64 entries. DST gap resolution searches at most 180 local minutes; a larger historical gap returns the typed nonexistent-local-time failure instead of unbounded work.

Synthetic checks cover default UTC behavior, business-day/calendar closures, overlapping pauses, DST spring gap/fall fold policy, folded adjacent windows, malformed and extreme inputs, empty calendars, reopening policy selection and configured resource ceilings. A 11 September correction expands `ambiguousLocalTime: 'both'` fold windows into their minute-precise UTC occurrence union, so it does not count an unscheduled gap between the two occurrences. The existing `earlier` and `later` endpoint policies and all three spring-gap policies remain explicitly covered. On a potential transition date, validated non-overlapping schedules bound fold detection and expansion to at most 1,440 local minutes each; emitted UTC pieces continue to count against the caller's interval limit. No remote action, migration, API, or external data was used.
